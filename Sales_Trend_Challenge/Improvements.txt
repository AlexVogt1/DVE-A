One effective way to enhance the detection of trending products is by incorporating sales velocity into the system. 
Sales velocity measures the speed at which products are selling within a given period, such as daily or weekly. 
By tracking the changes in sales velocity, the system can identify products that are not only increasing in volume 
but also accelerating in sales.

This approach allows the company to detect trends more dynamically rather than statically, capturing products that are rapidly gaining popularity. 
Once more data is collected, implementing sales velocity as a metric and maybe combined with historical sales data and external factors like seasonality, 
would make the system more responsive and accurate at identifying trending products.
